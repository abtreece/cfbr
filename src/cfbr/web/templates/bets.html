How it works
  • Bet the model's side when its predicted margin differs from the consensus line by at least {{ rules.threshold|num }} points (the Edge). The consensus is the median spread across sportsbooks in the newest line snapshot, offshore books included; projection and aggregator feeds are not books and are left out. Betting lines are never model inputs; the model's predictions are made first.
  • Each bet is recorded once, before kickoff, at that line, and never changed.
  • Graded at the recorded consensus line, {{ rules.odds }} odds, {{ rules.stake|num }} unit to win (break-even {{ summary.breakeven|pct }}). Every bet is a flat {{ rules.unit_value|money(false) }} unit from a {{ rules.bankroll|money(false) }} bankroll ({{ (rules.unit_value / rules.bankroll)|pct(0) }}, not compounding), so it risks {{ unit_risk|money(false) }} to win {{ unit_win|money(false) }}.
  • Best line: also graded at the best spread among the books a bet could actually be placed at, ties broken by the better price, at that book's own price (shown in brackets; {{ rules.odds }} when the feed has no price). Only {{ rules.pricing_books|join(", ") }} can set the best line{% if "bet365" in rules.pricing_books %} (bet365's lines come from a separate feed used only to price bets, so they never count toward the consensus){% endif %}. Every other book, including offshore books ({{ rules.offshore_books|join(", ") }}), counts toward the consensus but never sets the best line, so a bet can have no best line. In the Books tooltip, "(pricing only)" marks a line used only to price the bet, never in the consensus.
  • CLV (closing-line value) is how many points better the recorded line was than the Close: the median spread across sportsbooks in the last odds snapshot before kickoff (at most 12 hours earlier), or CFBD's post-game consensus when none qualifies. It is the first sign of a real edge, since win rate is mostly noise at small samples. CLV ¢ is the same gap in cents of fair price (bets settled from 2026-10-12).
  • Edge ¢ prices the edge in cents of fair odds from past final margins (a point through 3 or 7 is worth more than one through 15), assuming the model's margin is the true line, so it is as over-confident as the model. Cover % is a calibrated estimate fit on 2015–2021 covers; it found almost no signal and is reporting only.
  • Tiers group bets by edge size. Backtest is how often each tier covered the closing line in the 2015–2021 walk-forward backtest; no tier cleared break-even with confidence there.
  • Final scores are away–home.
