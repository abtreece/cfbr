Rules
  • Bet the model's side when its predicted margin differs from the consensus line by at least {{ rules.threshold|num }} points. The consensus is the median spread across sportsbooks in the newest line snapshot, offshore books included; projection and aggregator feeds are not books and are left out.
  • Each bet is recorded once, before kickoff, at that line, and never changed.
  • Graded at the recorded consensus line, {{ rules.odds }} odds, {{ rules.stake|num }} unit to win. Break-even win rate {{ summary.breakeven|pct }}. Dollar figures count one unit as {{ rules.unit_value|money(false) }}, so every bet risks {{ unit_risk|money(false) }} to win {{ unit_win|money(false) }}. Also graded at the best number available ("best line"): the best spread among the books a bet could actually be placed at. Offshore books ({{ rules.offshore_books|join(", ") }}) count toward the consensus but never set the best line, so a bet can have no best line.
  • Closing-line value (CLV) is how many points better the recorded line was than the closing consensus. It is the first sign of a real edge, since win rate is mostly noise at small samples.
  • Betting lines are never model inputs; the model's predictions are made first.
