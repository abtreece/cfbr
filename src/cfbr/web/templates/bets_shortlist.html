How it works
  • Every pick is a paper bet that also passes three rules fixed before any of them settled: FBS vs FBS with a line of 21 or less, not week 1; recorded at the week's first look; and one of your books offered the side at the paper line or better when it was recorded.
  • "Or better at" is the best number among your books at that moment. Lines are captured on Sundays; under each pick is what that book offers in the newest odds snapshot. Better means a friendlier number if you haven't bet yet. Worse means you'd need more points; a bet you already placed at the old number is then ahead of the market (closing line value, the best sign of a real edge).
  • "Crossed" names a key number the move went across: games end on 3, 7, 10 and 14 most often, so those points are worth the most. "Edge now" is the model's margin against the number now; under {{ "%g"|format(rules.threshold) }} points the model would not bet. Snapshots lag a live book, so check yours before betting.
  • Picks are in kickoff order. Bigger edges haven't covered more often in the holdout seasons, so they aren't ranked by edge.
  • With no books chosen you see the default set ({{ rules.pricing_books|join(', ') or 'any non-offshore book' }}), the list tracked below.
